Origin story
"We looked at Bloxstrap and thought — what if it looked actually fire?"

Bloxium started as a late-night side project. kpr.tekk had been using Bloxstrap — the legendary open-source bootstrapper by pizzaboxer — for months, loving everything it did under the hood. But he kept thinking: the UI could go harder.

So he opened a text editor, grabbed a reference to Bloxstrap's FastFlag implementation, and started building. The first version was rough — barely a loading screen and some toggles. But the core idea was there: a bootstrapper that felt like a real application, not a utility from 2008.

Six months of late nights, Discord feedback, and too much energy drink later — Bloxium v2.0 is here. Cinematic loading, 9 themes, synthesized keyboard sounds, proper texture removal, and a design that doesn't look like it came from a school project.

Big love to pizzaboxer and the Bloxstrap community for making this whole thing possible. None of this happens without their groundwork. 🫶
